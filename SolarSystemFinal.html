🪐 Popular Planet Types

🏃 Terrestrial Planets

Rocky planets like Earth and Mars have solid surfaces that space rovers can drive on.

🏠 Gas Giants

Gas giants like Jupiter and Saturn are made mostly of swirling gases and have cool rings.

⚔️ Ice Giants

These freezing cold worlds like Uranus and Neptune are full of icy materials and deep blue clouds.

Ordered List 💡 How to Start Stargazing

  1. Go outside at night
  2. Look up at the clear sky
  3. Find a bright shining planet
  4. Use binoculars or a telescope
  5. Have fun exploring!

Description List 💡 Space Terms

Planet
A large round world that orbits a star.
Orbit
The curved path a planet travels around the Sun.
Asteroid
A small rocky object orbiting in space.
Comet
An icy space rock that leaves a glowing tail.
